FILLED (6)
==========

President — Sebastian
  Sets the vision and holds the club's relationships at the top: Morrissette
  liaison, ratification, and senior sponsor and speaker relationships.

VP Sponsorship — Owen
  Builds the sponsor pipeline (Front Row Ventures, Ownr, MNP, Dragonfruit
  Ventures and beyond), negotiates cash and in-kind support, delivers on
  sponsor commitments, and secures member perks.

VP Outreach — Luke
  The club's face to the founder community: recruits guest speakers, builds
  and schedules the mentor pool for Sunday build sessions, and maintains
  alumni and Morrissette founder relationships.

VP Finance — Rishabh
  Owns the money: budget, membership fees, the prize pool, and sponsor funds
  and deliverables.

VP Operations — Sami
  Owns the physical and administrative execution behind the scenes: room
  bookings, catering, club portal and admin requirements, equipment, building
  access, supplies, A/V, setup and teardown.

VP Events — Ahad
  Owns what happens at an event and how it feels: programming, format,
  agendas, timelines, speaker coordination, judging flow and the run-of-show.

NOTE ON OVERLAPPING NAMES
=========================

Four open roles sit close to a filled VP role, presumably as owner and
executor. The names do not make that obvious to someone deciding where to
apply:

  VP Outreach (Luke)      vs  Outreach Coordinator (open)
                          and Speaker Acquisition Coordinator (open)
                              — Luke's role also recruits guest speakers
  VP Finance (Rishabh)    vs  Director of Financial Operations (open)
  VP Operations (Sami)    vs  Director of Logistics (open)
                          and Director of Admin (open)
  VP Events (Ahad)        vs  Event Experience Coordinator (open)

Worth deciding whether the open role descriptions should say who they report
to. The wording lives in one file: src/lib/execApplications.ts.
